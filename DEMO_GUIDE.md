# Classroom walkthrough

1. Launch **START_URBANFLOW.bat** and open the dashboard at http://127.0.0.1:8001.
2. In **Controls**, select **Classroom grid**, **Rush hour**, and **Fixed-Time**. Press **Start**. Use **1x** for close observation, **4x** for faster playback, or **20x** to advance the simulation rapidly.
3. Scroll over a road or junction to zoom toward it. Double-click an area to center it. In **Top-Down 2D**, dragging pans the map.
4. Open **Traffic** to inspect a vehicle or choose a junction. Click **Controls** or **Traffic** again to hide that panel.
5. Select **Adaptive**, then **Reset Scenario** to replay the same demand. Under **Traffic**, click **Open comparison** and then **Run comparison**. Review the ranked results and apply the recommendation if appropriate.
6. Use **Upload demand file** with `data/classroom-example.csv` to try custom traffic.
